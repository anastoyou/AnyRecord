Intelligent Camera

Smart Auto-Zoom

Tracks every click and keystroke automatically. Applies customizable spring physics, tension, and friction curves so your camera glides like a professional motion designer animated it.

Tension: 210 Friction: 24 Zoom Scale: 2.2x
Motion Polish

Cursor Physics & Sway

Eliminates shaky hand movements. Replaces raw OS pointers with high-DPI vector cursors, directional sway inertia, motion blur, and tactile click ripples.

120Hz sub-pixel interpolation
Exclusive Technology

Depthbake 3D Lighting

Powered by monocular depth estimation. Casts virtual 3D studio key lights, rim highlights, and ambient shadows across your footage for unmatched depth.

Real-time Z-depth normal mapping
On-Device Neural Engine

Offline AI Captions via Whisper

Generate dynamic word-by-word karaoke subtitles locally using OpenAI’s Whisper model. Zero cloud uploads, zero API costs, complete privacy.

“Create studio-grade videos in minutes.”
4K Canvas

Presentation Framing

Wrap recordings inside curated macOS wallpapers, mesh gradients, frosted glass padding, squircle corner radii, and deep elevation shadows.

Precision Editing

Multi-Track Timeline & Speed Ramps

Non-destructive split cuts, ripple deletions, per-segment speed ramping (up to 4x), and independent microphone & system audio track mixing.

DeepFilterNet AI

Real-Time AI Noise Suppression

Eliminate background chatter, fan whirr, room reverberation, and mechanical keyboard clicks in real time. Powered by on-device DeepFilterNet & DTLN neural networks—100% private, zero latency, and baked seamlessly into your exports.

Noise Gate: DeepFilterNet3 Attenuation: -35 dB Latency: 0 ms Live
